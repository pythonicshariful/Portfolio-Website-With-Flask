How I Work

Four steps. No guesswork.

Most email programmes fail because nobody checked the foundations first. Here's the order I work in, and exactly what lands in your inbox at the end of each stage.

  1. 01 — Audit

    Find what's actually broken

    I review your sending domain, authentication records, list health, and campaign history — then tell you plainly which of those is costing you money.

    You get: a written audit with prioritised fixes

  2. 02 — Strategy

    Agree the plan before any build

    We settle scope, timeline, and what success looks like in numbers. You approve it in writing, so nothing gets built that you didn't ask for.

    You get: a scoped plan with fixed dates

  3. 03 — Build

    Ship it, tested everywhere

    Flows, templates, segments, and domain authentication — built by hand in table-based HTML and checked in Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail before it goes live.

    You get: a live, documented setup you own

  4. 04 — Report

    Know what worked, monthly

    A plain-English report every month: what performed, what didn't, and the two or three things I'd change next. No dashboards you'll never open.

    You get: a monthly PDF with next steps
